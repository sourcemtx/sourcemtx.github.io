PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy for XPME

Last updated: June 2026

XPME ("the App", "we", "us") is a private, offline-first app. Your progress stays on your device, and we operate no account system or server that stores your personal data.

QUICK SUMMARY: We do not collect, store, sell, or share your personal information. There are no user accounts or logins, and your core XPME progress is stored locally on your device.

1. SUMMARY

  • We do not collect, store, or share your personal information.
  • There are no user accounts and no login.
  • All your data (skills, tasks, XP, achievements, avatar, settings) is stored locally on your device only.
  • We use no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking.
  • We do not sell or share any data.

2. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

XPME doesn't ask for your name, email, phone, location, or any identifiable info. We don't track you across apps, use ad identifiers, or run analytics/crash SDKs that profile you.

3. INFORMATION STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

To function, XPME saves your game data locally: hero name & avatar, skills/tasks/habits and completion history, XP/levels/streaks/achievements/quests, and preferences. This never leaves your device and isn't accessible to us. Deleting the App removes it; you can also reset it in Hero Profile → Manage Save. Optional backup files you export are handled entirely by you.

4. PHOTO LIBRARY ACCESS

Attaching a "proof of effort" photo is optional and only requested when you tap to attach one. The App does not upload, transmit, or retain the photo — it only registers that you added one for the XP bonus. You can decline and still use every feature.

5. NETWORK REQUESTS

XPME works offline but may load non-personal visual assets (decorative backgrounds, icon fonts) from third-party CDNs. These requests include no personal data, though the provider may receive standard technical info like your IP to deliver content.

6. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

XPME knowingly collects no personal information from anyone, including children. With no data collection and no accounts, there's nothing for any user to provide us.

7. DATA SECURITY

Your data lives on your device, so its security depends on your device's protections (passcode, biometrics, OS). Keep your device secure and updated.

8. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

Since we hold no personal data, there's nothing on our side to access, correct, or delete. You fully control on-device data and can delete it anytime by resetting progress or uninstalling.

9. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this policy and will revise the "Last updated" date accordingly.
