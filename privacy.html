PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 10, 2026

XPME does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. App data is stored locally on your device.

INFORMATION STORED BY XPME

Information you enter into XPME, including tasks, skills, XP, levels, streaks, achievements, character settings, and related progress data, is stored locally on your device.

DATA COLLECTION

XPME does not require an account and does not collect personal information, usage data, or tracking data through XPME-operated servers.

ADVERTISING & TRACKING

XPME does not use your personal information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

BACKUPS & DELETION

If you delete XPME or erase its locally stored data, your information may no longer be recoverable unless you previously created an export or backup.

FUTURE CHANGES

If a future version introduces features that collect or transmit data, this policy will be updated to describe those practices.
